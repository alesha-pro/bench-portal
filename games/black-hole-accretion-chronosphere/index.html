Field notes

The shadow
Rays that cross the horizon go dark. Light skimming the unstable photon orbit winds around the shadow and forms a narrow critical ring.
The two halos
Follow the warm plasma across the field. The far side of the same disk appears above and below the shadow as light bends back toward the observer.
Spectral shift
Orbiting gas beams light toward the camera on one limb. On the retreating limb the light dims and shifts toward amber.
Tidal disruption
Trigger the plunge to watch the blue-gold star shed an eccentric ribbon of debris. The gold-foil research probe tracks the event with a pulsed rangefinder.
