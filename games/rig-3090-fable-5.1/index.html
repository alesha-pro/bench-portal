Taking it apart

Every part comes from the photos: from the 20×20 aluminium angle and shelf sheets down to the zip ties on the chrome rod. 673 objects in the scene.

01 — graphics

4× RTX 3090

Three fans, 10,496 CUDA cores and 24 GB of GDDR6X on each card. Together, 96 GB of VRAM for large-model inference. Two rest on improvised stands, one stands on its side in the right bay.

02 — board and risers

Six slots, four risers

An ATX board with VGA, USB and Ethernet, turned ports-forward. The cards connect through x1 → x16 USB risers, and the tower cooler with eight heat pipes reaches the top rail.

03 — power and airflow

≈ 2 kW and four RGB fans

Two power supplies: 1200 W inside the frame and a Thermalright 850 W on its side at the back. Four 120 mm fans with RGB rings push air straight through the middle bay.

04 — engineering decisions

A Canon box and an egg carton

The top card sits on an EF 24-70mm f/2.8L lens box, the middle one rests its bracket on an egg carton, the third lies on cardboard. If it works, it is correct.
